## Full Backfill estimate

How long a Backfill of the whole Seed List takes as the pipeline runs it (#9): one company at a time, every Candidate that passes the name check gets a relevance call and the relevant ones a sentiment call, sequentially.

Candidates per company come from a real sample (`backend/test/fixtures/validation/candidate-volume.json`, recorded 2026-10-08): one in every 12 of the 258 Seed List companies (20 sampled), searched in en-US and he-IL for Q3 2026 with the profile the Seed List gives them. Mean articles found per company and edition: en-US 17.6, he-IL 0.5; 2 of 40 searches hit Google News' 100-result cap, so heavily covered companies are undercounted and the figures below are a floor for them.

The relevant share comes from this run, and the set is weighted toward ambiguous names, so it is only indicative: with no Candidate relevant classification would take 7 h 11 min, with every one relevant 14 h 39 min (median latency).

The Run fetches and classifies one company after the other, so the two times add up. The sample's window is a fixed quarter (Q3 2026) rather than the rolling 90 days, which is the same length to within two days.
